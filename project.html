Objective

Understanding Variable Treated as a Constant

Learn partial differentiation by treating other variables as constants. Compute partial derivatives, interpret slope geometrically, and apply to engineering problems.

  • Compute ∂f/∂x and ∂f/∂y step by step.
  • Visualise tangent slope along one direction.
  • Connect theory with interactive simulation.

Theory: Variable as Constant

Partial derivative w.r.t x: treat y as constant. Example: f(x,y)=x²y+3xy² → ∂f/∂x = 2xy+3y², ∂f/∂y = x²+6xy. Same differentiation rules apply (power, product, chain). Used in heat transfer, fluid dynamics, optimization.
